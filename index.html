The Art of
Measuring Time

Founded in Geneva in 1995, CHRONOS began as a collector's atelier dedicated to the pursuit of horological excellence. What started as a passion project has grown into one of the world's most trusted luxury watch retailers.

We partner exclusively with the greatest Swiss and European maisons, curating pieces that represent the pinnacle of mechanical watchmaking — each one a miniature marvel of engineering and artistry.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make exceptional
timekeeping accessible

We exist to connect discerning collectors with authentic, extraordinary timepieces from the world's finest makers. Through transparent pricing, expert curation, and unparalleled service, we make the world of haute horlogerie approachable without compromising on excellence.

02
Our Vision

A world that values
craftsmanship above all

We envision a future where mechanical mastery is celebrated, where each watch tells not just the time but a story of human ingenuity spanning centuries. Our platform is built to preserve and champion the traditions of Swiss and European watchmaking for generations to come.
